# Notes

How the main features work and where their data lives. The plan runs from Sat 26 Sep 2026 (kick-off weekend, week 0) to race day Fri 15 Jan 2027: 112 days.

## Data

- Every Firestore document is `{ j: JSON.stringify(data) }`.
- A profile's id is the user's uid, a log's id is `<uid>_<date>` and a weekly note's id is `<uid>_wNN`.
- Only members can read (`members()` in `firestore.rules` and `storage.rules`). You write your own documents and the admin can write anyone's.
- The plan ships in `public/plan-data.js` (`window.PLAN_DEFAULTS`). Edits made in the app are saved to `plan_days`, `plan_weeks` and `plan_meta` and override it.
- Photos: `photos/<uid>/<date>_...` in Cloud Storage, the original plus a small `_t.jpg` preview.
- Saves are optimistic: the page updates at once, writes are queued and retried and a local cache (`hx.cache.v2`) makes the page open fast on bad gym Wi-Fi. If the phone's storage is full or blocked the cache is dropped and the app keeps working from memory.

## Accounts

- Google sign-in, one profile per account, set up once with race format and division.
- The tracker is private. `members()` and `admins()` in `firestore.rules` and `storage.rules` list Firebase account ids (UIDs, from Firebase console → Authentication → Users), not emails. Keep both files the same; `TRACKER_ADMINS` in `public/config.js` repeats the admin ids so the app knows which buttons to show.
- Anyone else who signs in is refused by the rules and sees "This tracker is private" with a Look around button.
- Look around (`?demo`, `28-demo.js`) never starts Firebase: two made-up people (Alex, Sam) with logs up to yesterday, generated from the plan. `put()` goes to `demoPut()`, which keeps changes in memory only (no Firestore, no local cache or outbox). Photos are turned off.
- An admin account with no profile of its own (`adminOnly()`) is never asked to set one up and never shows among the people. It opens someone's log instead (the last one it picked on that device, otherwise the first person) and the top bar shows "Editing <name>".

## The day

- Warm-up, Accessory & core and Cooldown have Tick all / Untick all.
- Main strength shows the exercise name only; set rows come filled with the plan's reps and kg and the rest timer uses the plan's rest. ↑ ↓ moves an exercise. The order is personal and per day (`ord` in the day's log, plan indexes in display order) and sets stay stored by plan index (`l.str[i]`), so history and records are unaffected.
- Warm-up, cooldown and accessory & core can be edited *Just today* (in the log: `l.rm`, `l.xl`) or *Every day* (in the profile: `lists[g].rm` and `lists[g].add` as `[{t, from, to?}]`). Every-day changes start on the day they're made and Put back sets `to` instead of deleting, so earlier days keep their list and score.
- Finish session: Complete or Skipped, session RPE, minutes, notes and photos.

### Daily score (0–100)

| Day | Without Fuel | With Fuel |
|---|---|---|
| Training | Session 40, Work done 30, Warm-up & cooldown 10, Effort 10, Notes 10 | 35 / 25 / 10 / 10, Notes 5, Fuel 15 |
| Rest | Rest day 50, Active recovery 30, Journal 20 | 45 / 25 / 15, Fuel 15 |

Fuel only counts from a person's first nutrition entry (`profile.fuelFrom`), so older scores never change. Active recovery is any rest-day activity other than Full rest.

## Gym mode and HYROX mode

Full screen, today only. Both walk the whole session in stages: Warm-up, Strength, Run, HYROX work, Run, Core, Cooldown and a summary. A stage only shows when the day has it and a pill bar jumps between them.

- **Strength:** one exercise at a time in the person's order with big −/+ steppers. The weight carries over from the last ticked set. Done saves the set and starts the rest timer.
- **Run:** distance, time and RPE typed in, with the target pace shown.
- **HYROX:** `hxSteps(date)` turns the day's HYROX rows into steps. Continuous runs and stations become one clock with a tap per split (`l.hx[row].time`), sets become a set counter with rest, tests become a stopwatch and rest notes become a countdown.
- The clock is saved in the log (`l.hxc`), so it survives a reload or a locked phone and shows on other devices. When it finishes, `l.simTotal` is set and the day appears under Progress → Race → Simulation history.
- Station targets add the plan's Roxzone ÷ 8, because a station's split runs from the end of the run, so the 16 targets add up to the plan's finish (1:29:25 for Plan A).
- Each of the day's tests is recorded where it happens (a station, a time trial, a simulation's clock) and copied into the day's tests, so nothing is typed twice.

## Nutrition

`profile.nut = {kg, cm, by, sex, diet, goal}`; the daily log is `log.fuel = {w, p, pre, post, kcal, pg, kg}`. Targets come from `fuelTargets(pid, date)`:

- **Calories:** Mifflin-St Jeor BMR (or the latest InBody BMR) × a day-type factor: rest 1.45, strength 1.7, long run and HYROX 1.75, simulation 1.85, carb-load days 1.6 and 1.55, race 1.8. Lose slowly takes 300 kcal off and Gain slowly adds 300.
- **Protein:** 1.8 g/kg or 2.0 on Lose. At BMI 30+ it uses the weight at BMI 27.5.
- **Fat:** 25% of energy (20% on carb-load days), at least 0.8 g/kg.
- **Carbs:** the rest, never below the day's floor in g/kg: rest 3, strength 5, long run and HYROX 5.5, simulation 6, carb load 7 and 8, race 6.
- **Water:** 35 ml/kg + 0.6 L per planned training hour, rounded to 0.5 L.
- Under BMI 18.5 Lose slowly is turned off and a warning shows.
- Sources (listed in the guide): AND/DC/ACSM 2016, ISSN protein 2017, ISSN nutrient timing 2017.

## Training together

Train with… on Today starts a group straight away (no invite to accept), with either each person's own weights or the same for everyone. It first merges what anyone in the group had already done, then every save fills in the others: ticks, sets, order, HYROX rows, runs, minutes and status (kg only with Same). Never RPE, notes, photos, own items or test results. A copied log is marked with who filled it in.

`together/<uid>_<date>` holds `{g, in, group, ...}` so the rules can check it: X may change Y's log for a day only if X is `in` group `g`, the group's maker is `in` and lists Y, Y hasn't left and Y hasn't switched off "Others can add me to their group" in Settings.

## One shared plan

Everyone trains on the same plan and members can change it too: a member may write `plan_days` (`memberPlanDay` in the rules), while `plan_weeks` and `plan_meta` stay admin-only.

- **+ / − sets** apply to that day and every later day with the same exercise (1 to 12 sets).
- **Move a session:** two days swap, each keeping its own date. A day can move up to 3 days either side of its week. Days gone by, days anyone has logged, test days and race day don't move. Move back swaps again.
- **Catch up:** a session missed earlier in the week goes to a free day or onto another day's session. The missed day becomes a "Done later" rest day with no score.
- **Change exercises** (Settings → Plan): swap or remove a main strength exercise on every day from today on.
- When a day's lists change, everyone's logged sets, order and ticks move with their item (`ptRelogAll`). A member can't save a change that would have to move someone else's logged sets; the admin always can.

## "Are you sure?"

`sure.js` asks before keeping a value that looks like a slip: a test time under 0.4× or over 3.5× its target, a pace faster than 2:30/km or slower than 20:00/km, a weight over 2.5× the plan's (and at least 40 kg more) or over 400 kg. Nothing is refused; "Yes, keep it" keeps the value. It came from a 1 km test typed as `1:00` instead of `10:00`, which made the interval paces impossible.
